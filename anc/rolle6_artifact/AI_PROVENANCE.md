# Substantial use of ChatGPT

Most of the mathematical development and computational work in this project
was generated by ChatGPT (OpenAI), not merely edited or translated with it.
This includes many proposed reductions, most drafted proof arguments, search
and verification programs, exact certificate construction, analysis of the
remaining cases, the readable refactoring, and most of the manuscript.

Andrey A. Novikov selected and directed the investigation, proposed and
questioned approaches, requested checks for overlap and missed cases, required
incremental saving, and requested explicit disclosure of the AI contribution.
The work was carried out interactively during November 2026. The record does
not support exact model-version attribution for every step or a numerical
percentage of contribution.

ChatGPT is credited as a research and writing tool, not listed as an author.
The human author is responsible for any submitted version. Classical results,
the known quintic classification, and published inequalities remain attributed
to their original authors.

The full reference computation and refactored verification have been run.
These runs are evidence of reproducibility, not independent human review of
every mathematical reduction. No independent human audit of the entire chain
has yet been completed. The manuscript, code, exact certificates, per-word
ledger, and hashes are supplied to make such review possible.

The preprint is a prepared draft; no arXiv submission is implied by the
existence of this package.
